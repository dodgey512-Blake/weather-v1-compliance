范围与联系 / Scope and contact

本说明适用于网页评估版和Android测试包。维护者通过GitHub账号dodgey512-Blake发布，可通过项目Issues联系，请勿公开位置或其他私人资料。正式商业发行前须补齐真实法定主体、隐私联系人及非公开权利请求渠道。

This notice covers the browser evaluation edition and Android test build, published through the maintainer’s GitHub account dodgey512-Blake. Contact the maintainer through project Issues without posting location or private data. Commercial release requires the actual legal entity, privacy contact and a private rights-request channel.

本机数据 / Local data

无需注册。地点、计划时间和备注、偏好、跑步结果、决定、消息和实验事件保存在当前浏览器或Android沙盒。记录保留至删除，事件最多500条、决定最多150条、消息最多60条，天气快照为有限缓存。当前无云端账号、广告、分析或支付SDK，也不自动上传实验事件。冷热与完成情况由用户自行记录，不读取健康平台或传感器评估身体状态。

No sign-up is required. Locations, plan times and notes, preferences, run outcomes, decisions, messages and experiment events stay in this browser or Android sandbox until removed. Limits are 500 events, 150 decisions and 60 messages, with a bounded weather cache. No cloud account, advertising, analytics or payment SDK, or automatic experiment upload is connected. Comfort and completion are your own records; no health platform or sensor assesses your physical condition.

位置与网络 / Location and network

手动城市始终可用，四个预置城市的搜索在本机完成。主动选择设备定位后，经用途确认和系统授权获取位置。坐标先保留两位小数，再发送给NOAA / NWS查询天气与区域预警，并随所选地点保存。降低精度后仍属于位置数据。不请求后台定位，不保存移动轨迹。

Manual cities remain available. Search of four built-in cities runs locally. Device location is requested when you choose it, after the purpose notice and system permission. Coordinates are rounded to two decimals before being sent to NOAA / NWS for weather and regional alerts and saved as your selected place. Reduced-precision coordinates remain location data. No background location or movement history is requested.

NWS接收查询坐标、IP及请求信息，GitHub Pages接收网页访问的IP及请求。Android加载随包界面并联网请求NWS。未获商用资格的Open-Meteo免费地名和AQI端点已停用，空气质量来源待接入。第三方请求记录依照其各自政策处理。

NWS receives queried coordinates, IP and request information. GitHub Pages receives IP and website requests. Android loads the bundled interface and requests weather from NWS. Open-Meteo free geocoding and AQI endpoints are disabled pending commercial eligibility. An authorized air-quality source is still needed. Each third party handles request records under its own policy.

通知 / Notifications

通知需主动开启，拒绝后仍可浏览天气与应用内消息。只有应用可运行且刷新发现变化时才生成通知，关闭应用后没有远程推送或后台持续监测。可在设置关闭应用发送，在设备或浏览器设置撤回系统授权。手动城市不依赖非必要权限。

Notifications require your action. Weather and in-app messages remain available if refused. Notifications are generated only while the app can run and refreshes detect a change. No remote push or continuous monitoring runs after closing it. Turn app sending off in Settings, and revoke system permission in device or browser settings. Manual cities do not depend on nonessential permissions.

导出与清除 / Export and deletion

默认JSON导出省略坐标、快照、自由备注、事件属性与天气标识，仍包含城市和计划时间。日历文件含城市、时间和备注，生成前提示确认。分享前请检查，外部文件由相应接收方处理。

The default JSON export omits coordinates, snapshots, free-text notes, event properties and weather identifiers, but retains cities and plan times. Calendar files include city, times and notes, with confirmation before creation. Review before sharing; external recipients handle their copies.

重置经确认后清除此合规版本的记录、草稿、天气和资源缓存，停止应用通知并取消原生通知。旧版独立数据、导出文件和系统授权保留。可在系统设置进一步清除应用数据或卸载，网页可清除站点数据。本机清除不会删除GitHub与NWS的服务日志。

After confirmation, reset clears this compliance edition’s records, drafts, weather and resource caches, stops app notifications and cancels native notifications. Earlier editions’ separate data, exported files and system permissions remain. You can clear app data or uninstall in system settings, or clear website data in browser settings. Local reset does not delete GitHub or NWS service logs.

未来服务与权利 / Future services and rights

价格意向仅在本机保存，不收付款资料，不创建订单、试用或订阅。正式受众与年龄分级须按真实产品确定。接入商店、广告、云端或新数据方前，须更新目的、保存期、权利请求渠道、同意流程和商店数据声明。

Price interest stays locally, collects no payment details and creates no order, trial or subscription. Audience and age ratings must reflect the actual product. Store, ad, cloud or new data-provider integrations require updated purposes, retention, rights-request contacts, consent and store disclosures.
